# About

**Kyber CS** is a computer science book built from first principles — foundations for the cybernetics stack.

## Who This Is For

Engineers, researchers and hobbyists who want to understand Computer Science at the level where theory, systems, and intuition converge. If you've ever wondered *how* algorithms, architectures, and abstractions link together, this book is for you. This book is intentionally mathematical and code-driven, but in a way that aims for intuition rather than formal rigor.

## Who Writes It

**[RequieMa](https://github.com/RequieMa)** (魂麦) — engineer and author focused on statistical dynamics, complexity science, and cybernetics.

This is a **living book** — chapters are published as they're written. What you see today is a
snapshot of an ongoing project. Corrections, feedback, and contributions are welcome.

## The Thesis

Everything ties to one idea: **computation is the systematic manipulation of information under constraints — and understanding those constraints is the first step toward building systems that control them.**

Data structures and algorithms establish the basic vocabulary of time, space, and representation.
Computer architecture makes the abstract concrete — logic gates to pipelined CPUs. Operating systems
manage the fundamental tension between isolation and sharing. Networking reveals how systems
communicate under uncertainty. Databases show how to persist and query state at scale. Programming
languages bridge human intent and machine execution. Distributed systems confront the hard truths of
partial failure and coordination. Theory of computation defines the boundaries of what can be
computed at all.

This book traces that arc, chapter by chapter, always with an eye toward the cybernetics framework
that unifies the Kyber series — because to control a system, you must first understand the
computational substrate it runs on.
